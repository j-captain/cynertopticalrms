 

Here is the breakdown of the different eye care professionals to help you distinguish them:

1. **Optometrist (OD)**: This is the eye doctor you see for routine eye exams, vision tests, and **prescriptions for glasses and contact lenses**. They are the primary providers for delivering lenses.
2. **Ophthalmologist (MD/DO)**: These are medical doctors who can perform eye surgery, treat diseases (like glaucoma or cataracts), *and* prescribe lenses. You usually see them if you have a medical eye issue.
3. **Optician**: This is the person who **actually fits and delivers** the glasses or contacts based on the prescription from the Optometrist or Ophthalmologist. They are technicians, not doctors, but they are the ones who hand you the lenses.

**In short:**
*   You see an **Optometrist** for the exam and prescription.
*   You work with an **Optician** to choose and receive the lenses.
*   Collectively, the store is often called an **optical shop** or **eyecare center**.

If you're looking for a place that does both the exam and hands you the glasses, you just need an **Optometrist** who also has an optical dispensary (which most do).
